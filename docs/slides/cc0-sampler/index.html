cc0-sampler

A text-to-sample DiT (diffusion transformer), trained only on clean-licence audio.

Mick Grierson · Creative Computing Institute, University of the Arts London
Principle

Every weight has a licence we can state

Codec and text encoder: frozen. The DiT: trained here.

Data · Freesound

279k CC0 clips, 589 hours

CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) only. 2,762 excluded on clear evidence.

100 clipsModular Samples, 13.3%excluded
Data · synthetic

Generated data is ground truth

Aligned stems. Captions from the generator's own values.

Latent space

1920 times fewer frames

48 kHz stereo to 64 channels at 25 Hz, through a frozen VAE (variational autoencoder).

Codec quality

Better than MP3 at 64 kbps

It sets the ceiling: top octave, clicks, noise.

Model

One token per 40 ms frame

1024 wide · 16 layers · 16 heads · 326M

Objective

Rectified flow

xt = (1 − t) · x0 + t · ε
v = ε − x0

Straight paths. 50 steps to sample.

Sampling

Guidance limits variety

CFG (classifier-free guidance) 6 to 3: about 35% more diversity, same prompt match.

Training

200k steps in about 30 hours

Two 96 GB workstation cards. Batch 256.

v1 to v2

Longer, masked, a fifth synthetic

Trained from scratch on reviewed data.

v2 at 200k

Keeps time. Finds the note.

30 prompts × 2 seeds.

Speed

Hours, not weeks

v2 bar is an estimate.
